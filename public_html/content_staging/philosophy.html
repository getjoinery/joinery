Why Joinery exists

The SaaS industry treats your users' data as their asset. The Laravel ecosystem treats abstraction as the answer to everything. We think both are wrong.

The plumbing problem

Every new web project starts the same way. Auth. Sessions. Payments. Email. You've done it before. Your AI has seen it a thousand times. And yet it keeps breaking — because AI-generated plumbing is assembled, not understood. There's no coherent architectural vision. There's no one who thought through the edge cases. There's just code that looks right until it doesn't.

Joinery is 15 years of one person building the same thing correctly, over and over, until the architecture was solid. Not assembled — designed. Not inherited — owned.

The framework-on-framework problem

SaaSykit, Wave, Nana, Kitto. Every major PHP starter kit is built on Laravel, which is built on Symfony, which is built on... more abstractions. When something breaks, you're debugging three layers of someone else's opinions.

Joinery is a direct PHP application. No framework underneath. You can read every line. When something goes wrong, the answer is in the source — not in someone else's GitHub issues.

The commitments

These are specific, concrete commitments — not vague promises.

Source available, always.

You can read every line of code that touches your data. We will never obscure what the software does. If you have a question about how something works, the answer is in the source.

Self-hostable, always.

Every feature available as a hosted service, we will make available as a self-hostable option. If we can't, we'll explain why publicly.

Your data is yours.

Full export. No lock-in. If you leave, you take everything with you. We will never hold your data hostage or make it difficult to switch away.

No data selling. No tracking. No ads.

We make money from licenses and installation services — not from your users' data. We don't run analytics on your users' behavior. We don't sell access to your mailing lists.

Free for personal use.

Individuals, students, and nonprofits can self-host Joinery at no cost under the PolyForm Noncommercial license. Good software shouldn't require a budget.

Fair pricing, perpetual license.

Commercial licenses are one-time purchases with no expiry. All future versions included. We want to be sustainable, not extractive.

The business model

We sell commercial self-hosting licenses and installation services. Every revenue stream comes from providing a service — never from selling, sharing, or exploiting your data.

There is no venture capital. There is no pressure to "maximize engagement" or "monetize the user base." There are no growth-at-all-costs metrics driving product decisions.

We want to be sustainable, not explosive. We want to be around in 20 years, not acquired in 3.

Software that respects you

One coherent codebase. One architectural vision. Readable top to bottom.
